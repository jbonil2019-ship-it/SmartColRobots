BEFORE YOU PUBLISH
------------------
1. Video: put the file in videos/ with exactly this name:
     videos/SmartCol-expanded-smart-home-video-web.mp4
   Export it 16:9 (1920x1080 or 1280x720), under 5 MB, no audio.
2. Poster image (shown while the video loads):
     images/smartcol-video-poster.jpg
3. Product photos: replace the placeholder images with real photos of your
   products, then update the img src addresses in index.html.
4. Contact details: in index.html, footer section, un-comment the line
   marked "Add your email and domain here once ready" and add your real
   email and domain.
5. Legal / business details still to add: registered business name, VAT or
   company number, privacy notice, terms of sale, shipping, returns and
   warranty policy. These are not on the site yet.

HOW TO PUBLISH (simplest route)
-------------------------------
1. Buy the domain (e.g. on Cloudflare Registrar or Namecheap).
2. Upload the whole website/ folder, with index.html as the home page.
3. Connect the domain in the hosting dashboard.
4. Create the business email (Zoho Mail free plan works well) and add it to
   the footer.

Hero video: keep it light. A 20-30 MB file will slow the page on mobile.
